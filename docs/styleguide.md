# Undertone Style Guide v1.0
This document notes core branding principals to ensure consistent styling across social media, websites, print and application.

## Color
Undertone makes use of a core two tone palette.  Darker and lighter shades with variable saturation may be derived from these colors using OKLCH to ensure perceptive accuracy.

### Primary - Undertone Yellow
This is the core branding color for use in logos and highlighting.

- HEX: #d99b28
- OKLCH: oklch(0.7315 0.1413 77.35)

### Secondary - Undertone Blue
This is typically used for backgrounds in various shades, may exist in brighter colors as well when necessary following OKLCH perceptive guidelines.

- HEX: #1a3747
- OKLCH: oklch(0.3219 0.0451 234.7)

## Font
Primary brand mark is a U with an underline containing the brand name 'UNDERTONE'  in all capitol letters.  Both the primary U and Brandname use the font `Laco Pro VF`

Primary U is rendered at 948pts with Medium weight.
Brand name is rendered in all caps at 60pts and Medium wt with VA tracking of 100.  

The above pts provide a relative value.

## Logo Variations
In cases of high contrast or black and white print the brand should be displayed in either full black or full white with the logo name cut out of the underline to allow pass through of the background for consistent contrast.

In color a variation using the logo in `Undertone Yellow` with an offset shadow in `Undertone Blue` may be used when perceptually appropriate.
